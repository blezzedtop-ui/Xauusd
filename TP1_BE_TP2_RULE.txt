SignalX TP1 / TP2 execution rule

1. For positions above the broker minimum lot:
   - When TP1 is reached, 75% of the current position is closed.
   - The remaining 25% stays open and continues to TP2.
   - SL for the remaining position is automatically moved to the original Entry (Break-Even).
2. Applies to BUY, SELL, BUY STOP, SELL STOP, BUY LIMIT and SELL LIMIT.
3. Partial-close volume is normalized to broker volume min/max/step.
4. TP1 is processed once per position ticket.
5. A broker-minimum 0.01 lot cannot physically be split into a 75% + 25% position because 0.0025 is below the usual 0.01 minimum. For such a minimum lot, TP1 is treated as the effective final target and the SL is moved to Entry.
6. MetaEditor compile and live/demo broker testing are still required before production use.
