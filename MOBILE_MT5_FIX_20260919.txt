SignalX Mobile + MT5 Account Controls Fix — 2026-09-19

1) Mobile drawer:
- Removed mobile sidebar dimmer/overlay from the touch layer to prevent the screen from turning black and blocking taps.
- Sidebar uses a deterministic fixed drawer with high z-index and pointer-events only when open.
- Mobile navigation buttons close the drawer after selecting a section.
- Desktop layout is intentionally unchanged (breakpoint <=1100px only).

2) MT5 account controls:
- Existing account-level AUTO TRADE ON/OFF remains independent per account.
- Added independent AUTO PENDING ON/OFF per REAL/DEMO account.
- AUTO PENDING controls BUY STOP, SELL STOP, BUY LIMIT and SELL LIMIT.
- MARKET orders remain controlled by AUTO TRADE.
- Turning AUTO PENDING OFF cancels queued pending orders for that account and sends cancellation commands to the EA.
- Turning AUTO PENDING ON requires the account to be connected.
- Existing accounts receive pending_trade_enabled=true via backward-compatible migration to preserve previous pending behavior.
