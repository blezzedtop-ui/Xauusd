SignalX TP1/TP2:
1) TP2 is sent to MT5 as the final broker TP.
2) TP1 is stored per SignalX order ID in terminal Global Variables.
3) When price reaches TP1, the EA closes TP1PartialPercent (default 50%)
   if the broker's volume min/step allows a valid partial close.
4) Remaining volume keeps SL and TP2.
5) If a 0.01-lot position cannot be split because the broker step/minimum
   is 0.01, the EA safely keeps the full position for TP2.
6) TP1 completion is persisted per position ticket to avoid repeat closes.
7) Final MetaEditor compilation is required before live use.
